Do customers need to install an application?

No. Customers register through a merchant link and add the loyalty pass directly to their supported mobile wallet.

Does Just Loyalty support Apple Wallet and Google Wallet?

Apple Wallet is available today. Google Wallet support is planned and will be introduced in a future release.

Can I customise my loyalty pass?

Yes. Wallet design updates are included with the Growth and Pro plans and can be prepared during onboarding.

What happens after the free pilot?

We will review your setup with you and help you choose the Starter, Growth, or Pro plan that fits your business.

Can Just Loyalty integrate with my POS?

API and POS integrations are available with the Pro plan and scoped according to your existing systems and workflow.
